If you only remember three things

  1. Set your 3 2 1 countdown. No food 3 hours before lights out, no drinks 2 hours out, and screens off 1 hour out.
  2. Get the phone out of reach. Charge it outside your bedroom. A cheap alarm clock beats a phone on the nightstand.
  3. Feed your sleep. Tart cherry juice, two kiwis or Greek yogurt with pumpkin seeds are easy evening adds.

Don’t try to change everything at once. Pick one cutoff tonight and start there.
