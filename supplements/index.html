What about omega 3?

None of these multis include it. A fish oil or algae oil is the usual way to cover it, or eat fatty fish like salmon a few times a week. And for choline, food is your best bet. Two or three eggs gets you most of the way.

Get the most out of it

How to take what you buy.

  • Take it with a real meal. Vitamins A, D, E and K need some fat to absorb.
  • Take it earlier in the day. B vitamins can be energizing.
  • Split it up. Half at breakfast and half at lunch keeps levels steadier.
  • Give coffee an hour. Coffee and tea cut how much iron you absorb.
  • Mind your ADHD meds. Vitamin C and acidic juice can lower how much of an amphetamine stimulant you absorb. Ask your prescriber how far apart to take them.
  • Heads up before bloodwork. Biotin can throw off some lab results. Stop a couple of days before a blood draw.
  • Give it time. Nutrients don't work like a stimulant. Give it at least three months.
